SKYFALL CONVERGENCE Privacy Policy

September 26, 2026

This policy covers the SKYFALL Android app, package com.joskyl.skyfallconvergence, published by Trust The Wolf Tech Services Corp.

Information the app collects

SKYFALL does not ask you to create an account. It does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or advertising ID. It does not include advertising or analytics software.

The ritual video, audio, and session clock run on your device. The session length is chosen on the phone. That timing is not sent to us.

Internet

The app uses the internet only when you choose one of these actions:

  • JOSKYL Music. The song streams from Irys at gateway.irys.xyz. Irys receives a normal connection in order to send the video. That can include your IP address and basic request details. SKYFALL does not add an account or a personal profile to that request, and we do not receive a copy of it.
  • $JOSKYL TOKEN. This opens https://joskyl.com/ in your browser. That website is separate from the app and has its own pages and practices.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information with advertisers.

Children

SKYFALL is not directed at children.
